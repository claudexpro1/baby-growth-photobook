## Long description (both stores, max 4000)

Your phone is full of baby photos, and the ones that matter get lost. Baby Growth Photobook helps you take a small, meaningful set instead: one photo on days you choose, thinning out as your child grows.

**You set the rhythm.** Daily in the first month, weekly for a while, then monthly. Or anything you like. At most one photo per day, never any pressure.

**A gentle reminder.** Pick a time of day, or turn reminders off completely.

**Your photos stay yours.** Each photo is saved in your own Google Drive (or iCloud Drive on iPhone) as a plain, tidy file such as 00001_2026-10-10.jpg. No account with us. No ads. No tracking. We never receive your photos or your baby's details.

**A timeline that feels like a scrapbook.** Watch your baby grow with age labels like "3 weeks, 2 days", milestone cards and a slideshow. (planned)

**Share when you want.** Make a PDF photobook, send it with your phone's share sheet, or create an optional link to a folder for family. (planned)

**More than one child.** Each baby has their own profile, schedule and folder.

**Works offline.** Take photos anywhere; they upload later. (planned)

Don't want to connect cloud storage? You can keep photos only in the app, but we will remind you they can be lost if you lose or reset your phone.
